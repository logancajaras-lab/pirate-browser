❓ Frequently Asked Questions

🐌 It lags my computer when downloading!

This usually happens because video downloads use a lot of disk speed, causing a bottleneck if you're saving to your main Operating System (OS) partition. We highly recommend keeping your browser installed on a separate partition, or changing your download folder to a different drive. If you have plenty of RAM, you can use a tool like ImDisk to easily create a RAM partition for lightning-fast, lag-free downloads!

🔒 Is my data safe?

Yes! Pirate Browser is 100% open-source and transparent. It never connects to any servers run by the creator, and everything happens locally. You are the sole owner of your data.

🕵️‍♂️ Am I completely anonymous?

No! Pirate Browser is not an anonymity network like Tor. It provides excellent baseline protection against web trackers and browser fingerprinting, but it does not hide your IP address from your internet provider or the sites you visit.

🎬 How do I download streaming videos?

Simply play the video you want to download. Pirate Browser's built-in Media Sniffer runs automatically in the background. When it detects a valid video stream, a notification popup will appear—just click the download button on the popup to save it!
